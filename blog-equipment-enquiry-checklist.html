What to include in a municipal equipment enquiry

A checklist for getting a quotation ready for a general purchase, a GeM bid or another tender.

All guides ↗

Describe the work and the equipment

Give the equipment name, intended use and quantity. If you already have dimensions, a drawing, a chassis requirement or a list of accessories, include it. A short description of the operating site often explains why a particular configuration is needed.

Share the bid requirements

For a GeM bid or other tender, add the reference, submission deadline and relevant technical schedule. Flag any mandatory documents or inspection requirements in your enquiry so they can be reviewed against the proposed equipment.

Include delivery and handover details

Specify the delivery city and state, the requested delivery date and any access or unloading constraints at the destination. If the order will go to multiple locations, list the quantities per location.
