Trailers & tankers

Trailer-mounted or vehicle-mounted water tanker?

Use the delivery route, towing arrangements and dispensing setup to compare the two mounting options.

All guides ↗

Compare the two layouts

A trailer-mounted tanker is a towable unit. When you already have a suitable towing vehicle, share its details along with the route and working conditions. A vehicle-mounted tanker carries the tank on the vehicle itself. For that option, share the chassis details or state whether the vehicle needs to be included in the enquiry.

Describe the filling and dispensing points

Note where the tank will be filled, where water will be delivered and what is required at the outlet. A quote is easier to scope when it specifies the intended water use, target volume, outlet arrangement and any hose or dispensing requirements.

Check access at both ends of the route

List any tight turns, restricted entrances or uneven surfaces. Include the expected trip frequency and delivery location, so the mounting arrangement can be discussed in the context of the job.
