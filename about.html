HOW WE WORK

What guides each order

Build quality

The intended use guides the discussion about materials, dimensions and configuration. This matters whether the equipment is for a ward collection route or a public sanitation point.

Turnaround

We aim for a short turnaround. The timeline depends on the equipment configuration, order quantity and delivery location.

Pan-India delivery

We deliver across India. Share the destination and any site access constraints so they can be considered when discussing dispatch and delivery.

FROM REQUIREMENT TO DELIVERY

How to scope an equipment order

Define the operating conditions

Share where the equipment will work, what it will carry or handle, and any constraints on vehicle access or installation.

Review the configuration

Bring drawings, chassis information or technical schedules if available. Those details help clarify the proposed equipment and any items that need a separate discussion.

Plan dispatch and receipt

Confirm the delivery location, quantity and target date. For multi-location orders, include the quantity needed at each site.
